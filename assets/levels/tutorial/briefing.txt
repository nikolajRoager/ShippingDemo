Welcome to the tutorial, in this mission you will learn how to control your ships, how radars work, how to fight, and how to capture ships

There is a Russian oil-tanker escorted by a single Steregushchiy class Corvette somewhere to the North East, on the opposite side of Jan Mayern Island relative to you.
Corvettes are smaller and less powerful than your frigates, and you have 5 frigates, so this should be an easy first fight. But be careful, the Steregushciy still has 4 Anti-ship missiles, and if it detects you, you will come under attack!.
The mission ends when you have sunk the Corvette, and captured or sunk the oil-tanker, to capture the tanker you need to sink all warships, and get within 20 nautical miles of it, then it will simply surrender and stop moving.

But first, you need to learn how to play the game, to do that, either click the ? button in the top-left to open the guide, or go to YouTube and search for "ATypicalProgrammer Whirlwinds of Danger tutorial"

Press the ! button in the top-left of the screen to bring up the mission briefing again. Close this window by pressing the x in the top-right of this message.

Win condition:
* Sink the Russian Corvette
* Capture or sink the Russian oil-tanker

Loose conditions:
* All Ukrainian ships sunk
* All Ukrainian ships left the map
* The Russian ships left the map
* Ukraine sunk a Civilian ship (THERE ARE CIVILIANS IN THIS MISSION!)
