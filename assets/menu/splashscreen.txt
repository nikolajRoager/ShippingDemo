This game is meant to be played on a 1920 by 1080 screen.
If your screen is less than 1920 by 1080 you may experience graphical glitches

If you are playing on Windows 10 or 11 and get graphical glitches (like menus/UI falling outside the screen), try setting display scaling to 100% in the display settings instead of the default 125%, it usually helps.
